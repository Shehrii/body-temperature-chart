# Body temperature chart (°F to °C)

Every body temperature reading from 95.0 to 106.0 °F in 0.1 degree steps, converted to Celsius and labelled against the normal adult range (about 97 to 99 °F / 36.1 to 37.2 °C) and the CDC fever line (100.4 °F / 38 °C). 111 rows.

Printable chart: https://shehrii.github.io/body-temperature-chart/

Conversion: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9, rounded to 2 decimals. Readings with a full conversion page link to [Unit Equals](https://unitequals.com/).

## Files

- `body-temperature-f-to-c.csv` and `.json`: fahrenheit, celsius, reading (Hypothermia, Below normal, Normal, Raised not a fever, Fever, Very high fever), note, page.

## Not medical advice

Thresholds follow the CDC definition of fever. Ask a doctor about a fever in a baby under 3 months, one above 103 °F (39.4 °C), or one that lasts more than a few days.
